Client-Side Telemetry Conversion & Embedded Analytics

Embedded engineers developing on ESP32, Arduino, STM32, and Raspberry Pi Pico microcontrollers require rapid transformation of serial stream dumps into analytical formats. This tool offers deterministic, air-gapped parsing of Key-Value sensor pairs and NMEA GPS sentences into RFC 4180 CSV tables and formatted Excel spreadsheets without cloud dependencies.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my proprietary sensor logs uploaded to any remote server?

No. Parsing executes entirely inside your browser's local JavaScript V8 engine. No data is stored, tracked, or uploaded.

How does the Excel export format numbers?

Values identified as integers or decimals are cast to native spreadsheet numbers so formulas (SUM, AVERAGE) work immediately without formatting.
